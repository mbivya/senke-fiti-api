About Senke Fiti

Kenya's motorcycle-only online marketplace.

Senke Fiti is a focused online marketplace dedicated exclusively to buying and selling motorcycles in Kenya. Our goal is simple: create one convenient place where anyone looking to buy a motorcycle can find available bikes, compare specifications and prices, and contact sellers directly.

Unlike general classifieds platforms, which list everything from cars to electronics to household goods, Senke Fiti focuses entirely on motorcycles. That specialization lets us serve motorcycle buyers and sellers more effectively — with relevant search tools, motorcycle-specific information, and a more organised buying and selling experience.

What we do

  • Bring motorcycle listings together in one dedicated place.
  • Give buyers tools to filter by make, model, price, location, engine capacity, and condition.
  • Connect buyers and sellers directly — by phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Provide trust signals like logbook availability and verified seller badges.

What we don't do

  • We don't take commissions on completed sales.
  • We don't handle payments between buyer and seller.
  • We don't force subscriptions — listings are pay-as-you-go.

Who uses Senke Fiti

  • Individuals selling privately owned motorcycles.
  • Buyers searching for new or second-hand motorcycles.
  • Boda boda riders looking for affordable working motorcycles.
  • Motorcycle dealerships selling multiple models.
  • Businesses purchasing motorcycles for delivery and other transport needs.

Our focus is everyday commuter and commercial motorcycles commonly used in Kenya. As our inventory and audience grow, we'll expand coverage.

Our vision

To make buying and selling motorcycles in Kenya simpler by bringing relevant listings, useful comparison tools, and direct buyer-seller connections into one dedicated online marketplace.
