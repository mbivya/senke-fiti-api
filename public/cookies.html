What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow a site to remember you between pages and visits.

Cookies we use

NamePurposeDuration
session Keeps you logged in. HttpOnly (not readable by JavaScript). Up to 10 years or until logout

What we don't use

We don't currently use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or analytics cookies. If that changes, we'll update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the "session" cookie, you won't be able to log in to Senke Fiti.
