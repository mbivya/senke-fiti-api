Report a listing

The fastest way to report a specific listing is to use the "Report this listing" button on the listing page itself. That flags it to our moderation team immediately.

Report a stolen motorcycle

If you see your stolen motorcycle on Senke Fiti, use the report button on the listing and email report@senkefiti.co.ke with:

  • The listing URL
  • Your police OB (Occurrence Book) number
  • Proof of ownership (logbook, purchase receipt)

We hide reported listings immediately while we investigate, and cooperate fully with police.
